Platform Information

BGSBU Interacts is designed to connect current students and alumni while promoting mentorship, collaboration, career guidance, and professional networking within the university community.

  • Student and alumni networking
  • Professional mentorship and guidance
  • Real-time messaging and file sharing
  • Career support and resource sharing
  • Group discussions and mentorship sessions
  • Secure authentication and admin controls
{# -- University Info Modal -- #}
Baba Ghulam Shah Badshah University

Baba Ghulam Shah Badshah University is a prestigious higher educational institution located in Rajouri, Jammu & Kashmir, dedicated to academic excellence, innovation, research, and holistic student development. The university offers quality education across multiple disciplines while fostering professionalism, leadership, creativity, and social responsibility. With a strong commitment to learning, skill development, and career growth, BGSBU continues to empower students with the knowledge, confidence, and opportunities needed to excel in the modern world and contribute meaningfully to society.

About This Project

This project was developed as a Minor Project by a team of students from the Department of Computer Science & Engineering, COET, for Baba Ghulam Shah Badshah University, with the objective of creating a modern and professional Student–Alumni Ecosystem.
